# UX improvement list

Work through these ideas one at a time. The assessment was based on the app's screens and code; validate changes on a phone.

1. **Today:** Not doing the proposed next-step panel or reordering; keep progress and weight front and center. Empty workout states were clarified.
2. **First-use landing:** Give people who skip generation clear choices to add food, create a workout, or generate a plan.
3. **Workouts on mobile:** Focus on the current session and make exercises, logging, and replacement easier to use together.
4. **Food entry:** Organize around what someone ate and what they want to eat, while simplifying planned versus logged entries.
5. **Language:** Use consistent, clear names for workouts, sessions, plans, and creation actions.
6. **Navigation and visual hierarchy:** Give screens a clear primary action and make secondary tools easier to discover in context.
